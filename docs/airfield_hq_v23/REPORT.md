# Airfield HQ 2.3 compact review candidate

Branch: `feature/airfield-compact-layout` (based on `origin/main` at
`c445f57de724632e4f9c7b106080f3db0df1acf5`). This is a review candidate, not
an accepted final build. No merge, release or OTA publication was performed.

## Implemented dimensions and systems

| System | Authored / measured result |
|---|---|
| Runway | 75 × 18 m; five aligned, instanced 15 m authored 3D concrete slabs |
| Hangar | exported mesh envelope with guides/foundation about 22.5 × 21.055 m; approved interior source geometry retained in derived review asset |
| Construction zone | 28 × 24 m |
| Reserved interior clearance | 15 × 13 × 6 m; scene contract checks the reserved envelope, not a player walkthrough |
| Hangar doorway | 15 × 5.89 m; two animated sliding leaves and matching collision proxies |
| Tower | 8 × 8 m core, plus separate 4 × 8 m stair-access region |
| Apron / taxiway | separate 18 × 14 m apron, authored curved taxi surface with 18 m width, separate collision proxies and PSP plate patch |

The runway remains straight on one axis. The taxi curve connects the apron to a
straight runway-edge leg. The 12.44 m / 4.45 m P-47 reference gives 1.28 m per
wingtip and 1.44 m overhead at the hangar opening. These are static dimensions,
not vehicle clearance or flight tests. The runway remains 75 m; its adequacy
for P-47 takeoff and landing is unproven.

## Assets and source protection

`tools/airfield_hq/build_compact_airfield_hq_v23.py` derives new LOD0/1/2
hangar GLBs and an editable review `.blend` from approved HQ2.2 sources. New
runway, taxi and apron PBR LOD assets are generated by
`tools/airfield_hq/build_compact_paving_hq23.py`; the source review blend is in
`source_assets/airfield_hq_v23/pavement/`. Tower and PSP source assets are also
new HQ2.3 files. No approved HQ2.2 source files were edited; the protected
SHA-256 manifest check passed.

Material/base-color image links were corrected for four inherited materials.
Blender glTF export still warns that multiple image-texture nodes are connected
for some inherited materials. Godot material presence and UV contracts pass,
but the final in-engine material appearance has not been reviewed. The five
inherited hangar work lights are bounded in the placed derivative and disabled
by default. Existing Godot views still show clipped highlights in the hangar
and tower, so in-engine material/light parity is not accepted. A matched
light-on/light-off render has not been completed in this environment.

## Build and aircraft state

The existing building placement path includes the 2,800-point airfield,
single-instance guard, placement validation, payment-before-foliage-clearance,
move/rotate, repair/rebuild and sale-credit paths. Contract tests exercise
these code paths. An on-device touch interaction pass and visual HUD review are
not available here. The existing shop state reports the P-47 at 1,800 points,
but purchase remains disabled: `assets/p47_thunderbolt.glb` and the original
approved Meshy Pro model were not found. No replacement aircraft was created.
The saved markers/routes remain relative to the placed complex; taxi, takeoff
and landing have not been played.
