## Layout and measured area

The approved base perimeter measures **56.65 × 56.95 m = 3,226.2175 m²**, measured from `Data.COMPOUND` and the outer wall posts generated by `BaseProps._wall()`. The two-times cap is **6,452.435 m²**.

The new layout uses a **75 × 18 m runway** built from five 15 m concrete slab modules. Its conservative construction-area sum is **5,160.9318 m²**, leaving **1,291.5032 m²** below the cap. This deliberately counts the full hangar/tower envelopes, taxiway sections, apron, runway and taxi end coverage; overlaps are counted twice, so the actual paved/building union is smaller. The large empty gaps between those separate components are not reserved ground or pavement. `AirfieldHQ23.FOOTPRINT` is a camera-framing extent only; placement and the green/red preview validate and show each occupied region separately.

The complex remains freely placed through the existing build menu, subject to world edges, combat spawn clearance, the river, water, walls, bunkers and blocking nature collisions. The preview can rotate 90 degrees and pan/zoom the existing build camera. The normal ground-combat camera is unchanged. A production-world validation and visual capture found a clear rotated site at `(140, 0, 20)`.

See [compact_layout_audit.md](compact_layout_audit.md) for dimensions, module layout and the area definition.

## New 2.3 tower and modules

A separate, editable Blender 4.5.15 tower source and generated LOD0/1/2 GLBs are under `source_assets/airfield_hq_v23/tower/` and `assets/airfield_hq_v23/tower/`. The tower has a raised glazed observation cab, slim steel glazing frames, visible control-room furniture/radios/maps, exterior switchback stairs and landings with handrails, concrete/metal construction, aerial masts, wiring and mobile flat-color far LODs. Its authoring/render outputs include dedicated exterior and interior Blender captures. A historical point of reference is the [National Museum of the U.S. Air Force’s 1942 standard 8th Air Force tower reconstruction](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/195877/wwii-8th-air-force-control-tower/); this is a period-inspired game asset, not a replica of that specific tower.

The placed field contains five instanced 3D runway slabs with individual colliders and four shallow recessed expansion joints, distinct segmented taxi paving/collision, a separate 18 × 14 m apron, and the retained small PSP apron patch with eight plates. There are no new painted runway markings, berms, vegetation, tire marks, oil, decorative cracks or damage.
