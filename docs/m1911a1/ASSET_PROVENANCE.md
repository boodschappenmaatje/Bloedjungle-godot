- Original retained unchanged at `source_assets/m1911a1/740897__areniporgen__colt-1911.wav` (SHA-256 `d94343f3b0564fb79083e5f3c4f55ab41cd960ce4e21fecb2ff9ecf9f956c682`). The recording is described by its maker as a real Colt 1911 recorded outdoors; exact model/year not independently verified.
